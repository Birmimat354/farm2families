The Course

The Gallop is a mostly flat, casual, social run / walk around the greater Arlington area. This is not a race. Think Zone 2 long run (or walk) with friends. We start at Robbins Farm Park and meander through town with optional stops along the way to rest, hydrate, and socialize.

Assigned guides lead both the Runners and Walkers groups so nobody gets lost. Mostly.

Robbins Farm to Craft Food Hall

via Park Ave + Mass Ave + Minuteman Bike Path

Start at the lookout over Arlington, Cambridge and the Boston skyline, head up to the water tower, and then it's all downhill from there (literally). After the steeper drop down Park Ave it's a long, flat-ish shot to Arlington Center via Mass Ave, past the Boys & Girls Club and Spy Pond, then along the bike path with lots of chances to hand out gifts. Warm up and hydrate at Craft Food Hall.

Robbins Farm to Craft Food Hall

Zomsa to The Heights Pub

via Mass Ave

This is it, the final push!! The last and shortest leg is a casual stroll from Arlington Center, up Mass Ave, to Arlington Heights and our final stop at The Heights Pub. Runners and walkers share the same route for the finale.

Please note: this is an open course without markers (bring your sense of adventure and direction) or official timing (bring your own watch if you really care). You'll be running or walking through neighborhoods, trails, roads and sidewalks while navigating pedestrians, wild animals, bikes, cars, trucks, motorcycles, reindeer, etc. All participants must abide by state and local laws. All stops are optional and require personal financing if you want food or hydration beyond water; these businesses are not sponsors of or affiliated with this event.

Getting there & back

Parking

Plenty of on-street parking around Robbins Farm Park, along Eastern Ave or any of the neighborhood side streets.

Shuttles

We don't have any (yet). A ride share back to the start from any of the stops is a short 5–10 minute ride.

Bailing early

Totally fine. Just check in with a guide before you leave so we aren't counting heads for a ghost.
